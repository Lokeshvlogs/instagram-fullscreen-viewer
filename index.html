Questions

What's free, and what's Premium?

Viewing full screen, zoom, rotation, the tile strip, reels, profiles, Back/Forward, Save to Instagram and downloading originals are free. Slideshow and Image filters (presets, curves, auto enhance, filtered downloads) are Premium.

How does the free trial work?

Sign in with Google in the extension (or here). Premium is free for 30 days from your first sign-in — no card needed.

How many devices can I use?

Up to 3 browsers per Google account. You can remove old ones in the extension's control panel.

How do I cancel?

Sign in here or open the extension's control panel and choose Manage subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

What do you store about me?

Your Google email and a label for each device (like "Firefox on Windows"). Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see card details. Nothing about what you view on Instagram is sent anywhere.
